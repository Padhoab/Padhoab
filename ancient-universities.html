Ancient Indian Universities

Founder, Location and Important Facts for UPSC, PCS, SSC, Railways and Judiciary Exams

📚 Introduction

Ancient India was home to some of the world's greatest centres of learning. Universities such as Nalanda, Takshashila, Vikramshila and Vallabhi attracted scholars from different parts of Asia and played a major role in the development of philosophy, medicine, literature and Buddhist studies. Questions related to Ancient Indian Universities are frequently asked in UPSC, State PCS, SSC, Railways and Judiciary examinations.

⭐ Important Facts for UPSC and PCS

  • Nalanda University was founded by Kumaragupta I.
  • Vikramshila University was established by Dharmapala.
  • Pushpagiri University is associated with Emperor Ashoka.
  • Takshashila is regarded as one of the oldest centres of learning in the world.
  • Vallabhi University flourished under the Maitraka rulers.
