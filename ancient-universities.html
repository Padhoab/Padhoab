Ancient Indian Universities: Founder, Location and Important Facts

Ancient India was home to some of the world's greatest centres of learning. These universities attracted scholars from different parts of Asia and contributed significantly to the development of philosophy, medicine, literature and Buddhist studies. Questions related to ancient universities are frequently asked in UPSC, State PCS, SSC, Railway and Judiciary examinations.

Important Facts for UPSC and PCS

  • Nalanda University was founded by Kumaragupta I.
  • Vikramshila University was established by Dharmapala.
  • Odantapuri University was founded by Gopala.
  • Jagaddala University was established by Rampala.
  • Pushpagiri University is associated with Emperor Ashoka.
  • Vallabhi University flourished under the Maitraka rulers.
  • Sharada Peeth was located in Kashmir.
  • Takshashila is regarded as one of the oldest centres of learning in the world.
