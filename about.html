About PadhoAB

PadhoAB is an educational platform dedicated to helping aspirants prepare for competitive examinations through quality study resources, current affairs, quizzes, notes and exam-oriented content.

Our goal is to simplify learning and make reliable educational content accessible to students across India.

Examinations Covered

  • UPSC Civil Services Examination (CSE)
  • State PCS Examinations
  • Judiciary Examinations
  • Assistant Prosecution Officer (APO)
  • SSC, Railway and Banking Examinations
  • Other One Day Competitive Examinations

What We Provide

  • Current Affairs Updates
  • Monthly Magazines
  • MCQs and Quizzes
  • Study Notes and PDFs
  • Exam-Oriented Articles
  • Revision Resources

Our Vision

PadhoAB aims to build a comprehensive educational ecosystem integrating website resources, current affairs, quizzes, magazines, YouTube content and online communities under one platform.

We believe that quality education should be simple, accessible and useful for every aspirant.
