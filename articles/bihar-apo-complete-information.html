📢 Bihar APO Notification 2026

Bihar Public Service Commission has issued Advertisement No. 13/2026 for recruitment to the post of Assistant Prosecution Officer. A total of 300 vacancies have been announced under the Home Department, Government of Bihar.

⚖️ Bihar APO Selection Process

The Bihar APO selection process consists of:

  • Preliminary Examination
  • Main Examination
  • Interview

The Preliminary Examination is qualifying in nature. Final merit is prepared on the basis of Main Examination and Interview marks.

📝 Bihar APO Exam Pattern 2026

The Preliminary Examination consists of two papers:

  • General Studies – 100 Marks
  • Law – 150 Marks

The Main Examination consists of eight descriptive papers followed by an interview carrying 100 marks.

📚 Bihar APO Syllabus 2026

The syllabus includes General Studies, Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, Civil Procedure Code, Constitutional Law and several special statutes.

🎓 Bihar APO Eligibility Criteria

Candidates possessing an LL.B. degree recognized for enrolment as an advocate are eligible to apply.
