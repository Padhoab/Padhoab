Introduction

The Constitution of India is divided into various Parts, each dealing with a specific subject matter. These Parts provide a systematic framework for understanding the constitutional structure of India.

Questions related to Parts of the Constitution are frequently asked in UPSC Civil Services Examination, State PCS, SSC, Railway, Judiciary and other One Day Competitive Examinations.
