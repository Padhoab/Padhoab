MoRTH signs MoU with Tata Motors for replacement of old trucks and buses in Delhi-NCR

Why in News?

The Ministry of Road Transport and Highways signed an MoU with Tata Motors to replace old and polluting trucks and buses operating in the Delhi-NCR region.

Significance

  • Supports the Vehicle Scrappage Policy.
  • Helps reduce air pollution.
  • Promotes sustainable mobility.

Key Facts

Particular Details
Ministry Ministry of Road Transport and Highways
Partner Tata Motors
Region Covered Delhi-NCR

Indian Coast Guard inducts first Indigenous Air Cushion Vehicle

Why in News?

The Indian Coast Guard inducted its first indigenous Air Cushion Vehicle (ACV) in Goa. It has been designed and built by Chowgule & Company Pvt. Ltd., Goa.

Significance

  • Strengthens coastal surveillance.
  • Enhances search and rescue operations.
  • Promotes Atmanirbhar Bharat in defence manufacturing.

Key Facts

Particular Details
Force Indian Coast Guard
Equipment Air Cushion Vehicle (ACV)
Built by Chowgule & Company Pvt. Ltd.
Place of Induction Goa
