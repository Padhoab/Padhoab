MoRTH signs MoU with Tata Motors for replacement of old trucks and buses in Delhi-NCR

Why in News?

Significance

  • Helps reduce air pollution.
  • Promotes sustainable mobility.

Indian Coast Guard inducts first Indigenous Air Cushion Vehicle

Why in News?

The Indian Coast Guard inducted its first indigenous Air Cushion Vehicle (ACV) in Goa. The hovercraft has been designed and built by Chowgule & Company Pvt. Ltd., Goa.

Significance

  • Strengthens coastal surveillance.
  • Enhances search and rescue capabilities.
  • Promotes Atmanirbhar Bharat in defence manufacturing.

Key Facts

Particular Details
Force Indian Coast Guard
Equipment Air Cushion Vehicle (ACV)
Built by Chowgule & Company Pvt. Ltd.
Place of Induction Goa
